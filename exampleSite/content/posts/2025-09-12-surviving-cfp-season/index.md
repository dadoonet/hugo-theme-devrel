CFP season looks like productivity. It is often just twelve slightly different versions of the same talk, rewritten at 23:47.

<!--more-->

This fictional post shows how a DevRel overlay can mix **blog narrative** with **talk templates**.

## Keep one source of truth

Put the canonical abstract in `content/talks/templates/<slug>/`. Individual conference pages (`content/talks/YYYY/…`) stay short: venue, date, language, slides, video. When the abstract changes, you change it once.

## A checklist that actually fits in a week

1. Freeze the title before you freeze the demo.
2. Write the abstract in the language you will present in, then translate.
3. Record a 90-second pitch. If you cannot say it out loud, the CFP will not say it either.
4. Reuse the talk key (`talk: "Search that scales"`) so the theme can group occurrences.
5. Decline the events that would make the good ones worse.

None of this requires a CMS. Markdown plus front matter is enough, which is the whole point of this theme.
