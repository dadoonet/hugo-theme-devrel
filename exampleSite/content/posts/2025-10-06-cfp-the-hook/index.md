A Call for Papers is not a paper. It is a hook, an outline, and a submit button — in that order.

<!--more-->

This is part 1 of a three-post series shipped with the theme so `/series/` and the post sidebar have something to render.

## One sentence

If the reviewer cannot quote the talk in one sentence, they will not champion it. Write that sentence before the abstract, the title, and the demo.

## What this site does with series

Set `series:` in front matter. The overlay lists the other parts in the right-hand column (under the article on small screens) and links the series name to `/series/<term>/`.
