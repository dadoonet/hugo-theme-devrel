Part 2 is the outline: problem, move, payoff. Keep it short enough to rehearse on a tram.

<!--more-->

This middle post is the one to open in a review: the current part is highlighted, parts 1 and 3 stay links.

## Three beats

1. A problem the room already has.
2. A move they have not tried.
3. A payoff they can steal the same afternoon.

## Leave the demo in beat two

If the live cluster fails, beat one and beat three still stand. The outline is the talk; the demo is a prop.
