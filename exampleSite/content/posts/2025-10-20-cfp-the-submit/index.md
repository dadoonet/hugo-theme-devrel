Part 3 is logistics: paste the abstract from the talk template, then close the laptop.

<!--more-->

On this theme the canonical abstract lives under `/talks/templates/`. The blog series is the story around it, not a second copy of the CFP text.

## Paste, do not rewrite

Open the template, copy the language you will speak, submit. Rewriting per conference is how CFP season turns into twelve drafts of the same paragraph.

## Say no

A hook that fits Devoxx will not fit a 25-minute lightning track. The third step is choosing rooms, not filling every form.
