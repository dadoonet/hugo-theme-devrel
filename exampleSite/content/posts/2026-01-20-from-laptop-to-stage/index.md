The demo that worked on the hotel Wi-Fi at 07:10 will fail at 10:40, in front of 400 people, for a reason you cannot spell yet.

<!--more-->

This post is sample content for search, series, and cover cards. The advice is still real.

## Record a fallback

If the talk depends on a cluster, a SaaS UI, or a live crawl, film the happy path the night before. The recording is not a failure. It is how you keep the story when DNS decides otherwise.

## Make the laptop boring

- Airplane mode rehearsal, once.
- Fonts large enough to read from the back row.
- No notification banners. None.
- A local copy of the dataset, even if “the point” is the cloud.
